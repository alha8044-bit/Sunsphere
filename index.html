ABOUT THE PROJECT

About SoundSphere

SoundSphere is an interactive educational project that explores Earth's environment through sound. It connects scientific measurements with creative audio experiences to make Earth science more engaging and accessible.

How does it work?

The project retrieves selected measurements from NASA data services. The measurements are mapped to sound characteristics such as pitch, volume, and noise intensity.
